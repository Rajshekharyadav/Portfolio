Accessibility Statement

Raj Yadav aims to make itsraj.me useful to as many visitors as possible, including people using assistive technology or different devices.

Current approach

The site is designed with readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive image alternatives where available, keyboard-friendly interactive controls, labelled form fields, and logical page structure.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. New content and features are reviewed for clarity, keyboard use, and screen-reader support as the site evolves. Some third-party content or legacy material may not yet meet every accessibility expectation.

Need help?
